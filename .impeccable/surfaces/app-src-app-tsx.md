# Surface brief: Severity 1 (whole game)

Mode: Operate. Direction pinned by the user: completely modern, minimalist, white, premium, in the spirit of an Apple-style website. Pinned direction beats the roll. No copying of any company's marks or artwork.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Let the running system be the only loud thing on a calm white page. Refuses decorative worlds (print, ops-room, mascots) and heavy chrome.
OWN-WORLD: White page, F5F5F7 grouped surfaces, one blue accent, system font stack with tight bold display type, 16-26px radii, pill buttons, frosted sticky bar, soft diffuse shadows only. Status colours appear only as state, always with a badge shape.
STORY: The learner starts from one clear action, builds on a quiet board, and sees a machine's ring and shadow warm from neutral to red as load climbs, then reads the numbers.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Start screen: centered two-line headline, grey lede, blue pill "Start learning" with a quiet link, chapter cards below. Level screen: toolbox, board, metrics rail on a white page.
FORM: user-pinned direction, no roll (seed: none).
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
